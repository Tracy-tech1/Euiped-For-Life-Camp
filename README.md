# Equipped 4 Life Summer Camp website

Website for **Equipped 4 Life Summer Camp**, a nonprofit summer camp in Limbe, Cameroon that gives adolescent boys and girls the chance to step out of their normal environment and connect.

## Pages

- **Home**: introduces the camp, with camp photos as backgrounds and the camp logo
- **Gallery**: photos from camp 2026 and a link to the full album
- **Activities**: the six activity areas and a sample day
- **Register**: camper registration and volunteer application. Completed forms open WhatsApp with the answers ready to send to the camp team (+237 650 97 26 93)

## How it works

A single static page (`index.html`) with no build step. Photos and the logo are in `photos/`.

To change the WhatsApp number, activities, photos or gallery, edit the `CAMP` settings block near the bottom of `index.html`.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Go to **Settings → Pages** in this repository.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, select `main` and `/ (root)`, then **Save**.
3. After a minute the site is live at `https://tracy-tech1.github.io/Euiped-For-Life-Camp/`.
