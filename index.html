Our mission

A safe place to learn, grow and connect

Many young people rarely get the chance to step outside their daily routine. Equipped 4 Life creates that space: a structured, supervised camp where campers build practical skills, gain confidence and form friendships that last beyond the summer.

Campers spend time on digital skills, life skills and leadership, and take part in outdoor activities, whatever the weather. Every session is led by volunteers who care about young people's growth.

Digital skills

Computer and internet skills that open doors at school and at work.

Life skills

Good decisions, responsibility and self-care for everyday life.

Connection

Team activities that build respect, friendship and belonging.

Outdoor experience

Excursions and time outdoors, away from the usual environment.

Programme

Camp activities

The programme balances learning with experience. Campers rotate through six areas, each led by a trained volunteer and adapted to the campers' ages.

A day at camp

Sample daily schedule

Each day is structured and supervised from arrival to pick-up. Final times are shared with families after registration. Sample

8:30Arrival and morning assembly: welcome, notices and the day's theme
9:30Session 1: digital skills or life skills
11:00Break
11:30Session 2: leadership and public speaking
13:00Lunch
14:00Outdoor activity: excursion, sports or team challenge
15:30Reflection and pick-up
